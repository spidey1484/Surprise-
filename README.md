# Our First Year

A responsive one-year relationship anniversary website. Open `index.html` in any modern browser.

## Make it yours in 3 minutes

1. In `index.html`, replace every `her name` and `your name` with your names.
2. Change the date near the top of the page (`01 · 09 · 2025 — 01 · 09 · 2026`) and the `anniversaryStart` date in `script.js`.
3. Add your photographs by replacing the two image URLs in `style.css`:
   - `.memory-image-1` is the tall first photo.
   - `.memory-image-2` is the wide second photo.
4. Personalize the timeline and the letter in `index.html` with your own memories.

## Share it

You can send the folder to someone to open locally, or upload its contents to Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages, or any web host. Keep `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js` together.
